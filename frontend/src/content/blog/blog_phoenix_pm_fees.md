## The Short Answer: Average Fees in Phoenix

According to proprietary data from RankMyPropertyManager, the average property management fee in Phoenix, AZ typically ranges between **7% and 10% of the monthly collected rent**. 

Because of the highly competitive nature of the Phoenix market, baseline management fees are slightly lower than the national average. However, the monthly management fee is only one piece of the puzzle. The true cost of property management is dictated by the "hidden" fees buried in your contract.


## Breakdown of Standard Phoenix PM Fees

When comparing local Phoenix property managers, you need to account for four distinct fee structures:

### 1. The Leasing Fee (Tenant Placement)
This is the fee charged for aggressively marketing your property, showing it to prospective tenants, running strict background checks, and executing the lease. 
*   **Phoenix Average:** 50% to 75% of one full month's rent (or a flat fee of $300 - $600).
*   *Warning:* If a company charges a highly discounted monthly management fee (e.g., 5-6%), they almost always make up for it by charging a full 100% leasing fee every time a tenant turns over. 

### 2. The Monthly Management Fee
This covers day-to-day operations: collecting rent, handling 24/7 maintenance calls (especially critical summer AC failures), and dealing with tenant disputes.
*   **Phoenix Average:** 7% - 10% (or a flat fee of $90 - $130 per month).
*   *Pro-Tip:* Ensure this fee is based on "Rent Collected," not "Rent Due." You should never pay a management fee on a vacant property.

### 3. Lease Renewal Fees
When a great tenant decides to stay another year, the property manager handles the lease renewal and market rent analysis.
*   **Phoenix Average:** $150 - $300 flat fee.

### 4. Maintenance Markups
Some property management companies will add a surcharge (usually 10%) on top of any vendor invoices for coordinating repairs. 
*   *The Standard:* The highest-rated property managers in Phoenix are transparent about their vendor networks and do **not** charge hidden maintenance markups. They pass the raw invoice directly to the landlord.
