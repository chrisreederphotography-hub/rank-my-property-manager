However, the monthly management fee is only one piece of the puzzle. The true cost of property management is dictated by the "hidden" fees in your contract.


## Breakdown of Standard Austin PM Fees

When comparing local Austin property managers, you need to account for four distinct fee structures:

### 1. The Leasing Fee (Tenant Placement)
This is the fee charged for marketing your property, showing it to prospective tenants, running background checks, and signing the lease. 
*   **Austin Average:** 50% to 100% of one full month's rent.
*   *Warning:* If a company charges a low 6% monthly management fee, they almost always make up for it by charging a full 100% leasing fee. 

### 2. The Monthly Management Fee
This covers day-to-day operations: collecting rent, handling maintenance calls, and dealing with tenant disputes.
*   **Austin Average:** 8% - 10% (or a flat fee of $99 - $150 per month).
*   *Pro-Tip:* Ensure this fee is based on "Rent Collected," not "Rent Due." You should never pay a management fee on a vacant property.

### 3. Lease Renewal Fees
When a great tenant decides to stay another year, the property manager handles the lease renewal and market rent analysis.
*   **Austin Average:** $250 - $400 flat fee.

### 4. Maintenance Markups
Some property management companies will add a surcharge (usually 10%) on top of any vendor invoices for coordinating repairs. 
*   *The Standard:* The highest-rated property managers in Austin do **not** charge maintenance markups. They pass the raw invoice directly to the landlord.
