## What Makes a Dallas Property Manager Great?

Before you sign a contract, the best property management companies in Texas will excel in these three areas:

### 1. Rigorous, Localized Tenant Screening
A bad tenant can cost you thousands of dollars in property damage and legal fees. Top Dallas managers run multi-point screenings, checking credit, criminal history, and previous Texas eviction records. They understand the local DFW demographics and ensure you only place highly reliable tenants in your home.

### 2. Transparent Flat-Fee Pricing Models
The Dallas market is highly competitive, and the pricing models reflect that. Because average rent prices are strong, giving away 10% of your gross income every month drastically reduces your ROI. The best property managers in DFW are now offering transparent, flat monthly fees (often between $99 and $149), meaning you keep more of your money.

### 3. Rapid Eviction and Legal Compliance
While you hope you never need it, the ability to rapidly execute an eviction is crucial. Under Texas law, the standard Notice to Vacate is 3 days. A top-rated Dallas PM knows exactly how to navigate the local Justice of the Peace courts in Dallas or Tarrant County flawlessly, minimizing your vacancy time and legal headaches.

## Avoid the "Discount" Trap

When searching for the best property manager in Dallas, be wary of the "Discount Trap." 

If a company advertises a suspiciously low monthly management fee (e.g., 4% or 5%), read the fine print. They almost always make up the difference by charging astronomical "Leasing Fees" (often 100% of the first month's rent) or adding a 10% to 15% hidden surcharge onto every single maintenance invoice. 

The highest-rated companies are 100% transparent about their fee structures upfront.
