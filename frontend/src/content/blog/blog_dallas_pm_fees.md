## The Short Answer: Average Fees in Dallas

According to proprietary data from RankMyPropertyManager, the average property management fee in Dallas, TX generally sits between **7% and 10% of the monthly collected rent**. 

Because of the highly competitive nature of the DFW market, baseline management fees are slightly lower than the national average. However, the monthly management fee is only one piece of the puzzle. The true cost of property management is dictated by the "hidden" fees buried in your contract.


## Breakdown of Standard Dallas PM Fees

When comparing local Dallas property managers, you need to account for four distinct fee structures:

### 1. The Leasing Fee (Tenant Placement)
This is the fee charged for aggressively marketing your property, showing it to prospective tenants, running strict background checks, and executing the lease. 
*   **Dallas Average:** 50% to 100% of one full month's rent.
*   *Warning:* If a company charges a highly discounted monthly management fee (e.g., 5-6%), they almost always make up for it by charging a full 100% leasing fee every time a tenant turns over. 

### 2. The Monthly Management Fee
This covers day-to-day operations: collecting rent, handling 24/7 maintenance calls, and dealing with tenant disputes.
*   **Dallas Average:** 7% - 10% (or a flat fee of $99 - $149 per month).
*   *Pro-Tip:* Ensure this fee is based on "Rent Collected," not "Rent Due." You should never pay a management fee on a vacant property.

### 3. Lease Renewal Fees
When a great tenant decides to stay another year, the property manager handles the lease renewal and market rent analysis.
*   **Dallas Average:** $200 - $350 flat fee.

### 4. Maintenance Markups
Some property management companies will add a surcharge (usually 10%) on top of any vendor invoices for coordinating repairs. 
*   *The Standard:* The highest-rated property managers in Dallas are transparent about their vendor networks and do **not** charge hidden maintenance markups. They pass the raw invoice directly to the landlord.
