## Breakdown of Standard Miami PM Fees

When comparing local Miami property managers, you need to account for four distinct fee structures:

### 1. The Leasing Fee (Tenant Placement)
This is the fee charged for marketing your property, showing it to prospective tenants, running strict background checks, and executing the lease. 
*   **Miami Average:** 50% to 100% of one full month's rent.
*   *Warning:* If a company charges a heavily discounted monthly management fee (e.g., 6%), they almost always make up for it by charging a full 100% leasing fee every time a tenant turns over. 

### 2. The Monthly Management Fee
This covers day-to-day operations: collecting rent, handling 24/7 maintenance calls, and dealing with tenant disputes.
*   **Miami Average:** 8% - 12% (or a flat fee of $125 - $175 per month).
*   *Pro-Tip:* Ensure this fee is based on "Rent Collected," not "Rent Due." You should never pay a management fee on a vacant property, especially during the slow rental season.

### 3. Lease Renewal Fees
When a great tenant decides to stay another year, the property manager handles the lease renewal and market rent analysis.
*   **Miami Average:** $250 - $450 flat fee.

### 4. Maintenance Markups & Hurricane Prep
Some property management companies will add a surcharge (usually 10%) on top of any vendor invoices for coordinating repairs. In Miami, you must also ask if they charge extra fees for hurricane shutter installation and storm prep. 
*   *The Standard:* The highest-rated property managers in Miami are transparent about their vendor networks and do **not** charge hidden maintenance markups.
