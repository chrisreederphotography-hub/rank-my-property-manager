## The Short Answer: Average Fees in Denver

According to proprietary data from RankMyPropertyManager, the average property management fee in Denver, CO typically ranges between **8% and 10% of the monthly collected rent**. 

Because of the specialized knowledge required to manage Colorado properties year-round, these baseline management fees are slightly higher than in some flat-weather states. However, the monthly management fee is only one piece of the puzzle. The true cost of property management is dictated by the "hidden" fees buried in your contract.

## Breakdown of Standard Denver PM Fees

When comparing local Denver property managers, you need to account for four distinct fee structures:

### 1. The Leasing Fee (Tenant Placement)
This is the fee charged for aggressively marketing your property, showing it to prospective tenants, running strict background checks, and executing the lease. 
*   **Denver Average:** 50% to 100% of one full month's rent.
*   *Warning:* If a company charges a highly discounted monthly management fee (e.g., 5-6%), they almost always make up for it by charging a full 100% leasing fee every time a tenant turns over. 

### 2. The Monthly Management Fee
This covers day-to-day operations: collecting rent, handling 24/7 maintenance calls, and dealing with tenant disputes.
*   **Denver Average:** 8% - 10% (or a flat fee of $110 - $160 per month).
*   *Pro-Tip:* Ensure this fee is based on "Rent Collected," not "Rent Due." You should never pay a management fee on a vacant property.

### 3. Lease Renewal Fees
When a great tenant decides to stay another year, the property manager handles the lease renewal and market rent analysis.
*   **Denver Average:** $200 - $350 flat fee.

### 4. Maintenance Markups
Some property management companies will add a surcharge (usually 10%) on top of any vendor invoices for coordinating repairs. 
*   *The Standard:* The highest-rated property managers in Denver are transparent about their vendor networks and do **not** charge hidden maintenance markups. They pass the raw invoice directly to the landlord.
