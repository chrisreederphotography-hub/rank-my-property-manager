# When Should You Hire a Property Manager vs. DIY?

The debate is as old as real estate investing itself: Should you manage the property yourself to save 10%, or hire a professional to reclaim your time?

While managing 1 or 2 doors yourself is manageable, there is a clear "breaking point" where DIY landlords actually start losing money. Here is how to know when it's time to hire a pro.

## Sign 1: You Live Further Than 45 Minutes Away
Proximity is everything. If a pipe bursts at 2 AM or a tenant locks themselves out, can you be there in under an hour? If your rental property is out-of-state or multiple towns over, managing it yourself is a massive liability. 

## Sign 2: You Value Your Hourly Rate
Calculate your hourly rate at your day job or business. Let's say your time is worth $100/hour. If you spend 5 hours a month answering tenant calls, coordinating plumbers, and doing bookkeeping, that costs you $500 in lost time. 
If a property manager charges $150/month to do the same work, you are mathematically losing money by doing it yourself.

## Sign 3: You Hate Confrontation
Being a landlord requires tough conversations. 
- Charging late fees.
- Serving eviction notices.
- Telling a tenant they aren't getting their full deposit back.
If you avoid confrontation or find yourself letting tenants slide on late rent, you need a professional buffer. Property managers enforce the lease strictly and legally.

## Sign 4: You Want to Scale
You can manually manage 3 properties. You cannot manually manage 30 properties while working a full-time job. If your goal is to build a massive real estate portfolio, a property management team is a mandatory piece of your infrastructure.
