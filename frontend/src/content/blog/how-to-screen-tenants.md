# How to Screen Tenants: A Landlord's Checklist

A bad tenant can cost thousands in lost rent and property damage. A great tenant pays on time, treats the property with respect, and stays for years. 

Here is the ultimate 2026 checklist for screening tenants effectively and legally.

## 1. Set Clear Rental Criteria
Before you accept a single application, write down your minimum requirements. This protects you from Fair Housing complaints.
- **Income:** Must earn 3x the monthly rent.
- **Credit Score:** Minimum 650.
- **History:** No evictions in the last 5 years.

## 2. Run a Comprehensive Background Check
Never skip this step. Use a reputable service (or have your property manager do it) to pull:
- National criminal history.
- Eviction database records.
- Credit reports.

## 3. Verify Income and Employment
Fake pay stubs are increasingly common in 2026. 
- **Action:** Ask for 2-3 months of recent bank statements instead of just pay stubs.
- **Action:** Call their employer to verify active employment status.

## 4. Call Previous Landlords
Don't just call their *current* landlord (who might lie just to get rid of them). Call their *previous* landlord. 
- Ask: "Did they pay on time?"
- Ask: "Did they leave the property in good condition?"
- Ask: "Would you rent to them again?"
